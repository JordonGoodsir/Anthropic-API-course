# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Runner - Carb-Loading & Training Day

Here is a one-day meal plan for a vegetarian marathon runner preparing for a 20km long training run:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (110g), Fat (80g), Carbs (440g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), raisins (40g), and sunflower seeds (20g). Whole milk (250ml).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Pre-Run Snack (8:30 AM):** White bread toast (60g) with jam (30g) and a sports drink (250ml, 6% carbs).
    * Protein: 4g, Fat: 2g, Carbs: 65g

* **Post-Run Recovery Meal (11:30 AM):** Chickpea pasta (80g dry weight) with marinara sauce (150g), steamed zucchini (100g), and grated cheese (30g). Whole wheat bread (50g).
    * Protein: 22g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 85g

* **Lunch Snack (1:30 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g, plain) with honey (25g) and granola (40g, nut-free).
    * Protein: 15g, Fat: 6g, Carbs: 50g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Rice cakes (40g) with avocado (80g) and tomato (50g).
    * Protein: 5g, Fat: 10g, Carbs: 35g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Lentil curry (150g cooked lentils) with basmati rice (100g cooked), spinach (100g), and vegetable broth (200ml). Naan bread (60g).
    * Protein: 30g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Smoothie made with banana (100g), oat milk (200ml), and protein powder (25g).
    * Protein: 16g, Fat: 2g, Carbs: 35g

---

**Key Features:** This meal plan prioritizes easily digestible carbohydrates for energy loading, with strategic timing before and after your 10:00 AM run. The elevated carb intake (440g) supports glycogen repletion for endurance training. All meals are vegetarian and nut-free, with adequate protein distributed throughout the day to support muscle recovery.
